      Ask

      Select a question on any page and click Ask (the speech bubble) next to it, or press Alt+Shift+Q: a short answer shows in the bottom-left corner. × hides the button for that selection.

      Only the selected text is sent, straight to your AI provider, with your Knowledge text while its switch is on. Text in fields, single words (like links or passwords) and mostly numbers (like card numbers) are never sent. The switch turns the button and the shortcut off on all pages.

      Knowledge

      Sent to your AI provider with every question you ask, up to 5,000 characters: a few lines about you (what you study, your level, the language you want) or your study materials. A language set in settings wins over one asked for here.

      It saves as you type and stays on this device. The switch stops sending it without deleting it.

      Voice

      Switch it on, then click a text field on a page and the microphone below it. Speak, then click again: the text is typed where the cursor is. Esc cancels. With a language set in settings, speak in that language; on Automatic, any language is recognized.

      The microphone is on only while the button is red (the toolbar icon shows “rec”). The audio goes straight to your AI provider's speech-to-text model and Pilbot keeps none of it. Fields for passwords, card numbers and codes get no button.

      Summary

      When you open a page with enough text, a small bar shows at the top: click the summary icon for a summary of the page in 2–3 sentences, or × to close the bar.

      Only the page title and its readable text are sent to your AI provider, after you click: menus, ads, comments, forms, your own typing and hidden parts are left out, and long pages are shortened. Your Knowledge text is not sent.
